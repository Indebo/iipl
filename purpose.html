/Purpose

We are Indebo. We design travel — to meet expectations, derive fun, achieve what you set out to do. To learn, and unlearn. To lose yourself, and then, find yourself again.

If you fail to design, you are designed to fail.

Travel is a verb, not an object you buy — only something you experience. Sometimes, one that brings you home again, longingly. We design deliberately. Meaningfully. Purposefully.

We care about the environment, and how over-tourism and careless travel feed climate change. About gender equality — because each of us holds up half the earth.

We're a for-profit company. We value our time. And yours. We are IndeBo: independent, indelible.

We grow nuts, coconut and cashew, spices, rubber — and lately, we're learning about dragonfruit. We've been around since 1979, long enough. We belong to ourselves, and to the cause, not to shareholders. We're based in India, by choice — that doesn't limit where we go.

What inspires us: thinking big and scalable, executing small and nimble, bringing real value. We welcome back our alumni, and those they send our way, with gratitude.

— Team Indebo
